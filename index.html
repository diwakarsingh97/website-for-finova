05 / Questions

Things you might want to know.

What is FINOVA?+
FINOVA is a personal finance management platform designed to bring expenses, budgets, savings goals, wallets, recurring payments and financial insights into one experience.
Who is FINOVA for?+
Anyone who wants a clearer view of their money — students, professionals, families, freelancers or anyone trying to build better financial habits.
Can I manage multiple wallets?+
FINOVA is designed around multiple wallets and accounts so you can keep different sources of money organised without losing the bigger picture.
Does FINOVA replace a bank?+
No. FINOVA is a financial management tool. It helps you understand and organise your finances; it is not a bank or a replacement for regulated financial services.
